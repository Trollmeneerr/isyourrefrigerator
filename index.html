01 // MY PRINCIPLES

Overview

How I keep IsYourRefrigerator online for you & me, why I run the freezers for free, and of course why IsYourRefrigerator is online.

PRINCIPLE #1

No Cost, Always Free

No paywalls, no monthly subscriptions, and no surprise invoices. Free hosting for my friends, experiments, side projects, and fun little web tools. You can even request a free custom egg for your server if it doesn't require much time to setup and if I have time.

Always free for me & friends

PRINCIPLE #2

Fair Share Hosting

If outsourcing dedicated freezer resources, just keep it fair. At least pay for the service and the electricity so I can keep my refrigerator running for everyone. It won't be much per month but it will keep the freezer cold.

Always keep the refrigerator online

PRINCIPLE #3

Hosted with Love

I do this for the love of the game, for the convenience, and will never save any logs about you or anyone. I don't collect any personal information about you, your friends, your projects, or your servers.

Always hosting with love
