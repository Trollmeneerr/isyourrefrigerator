● Zero Tracking Policy

This website (IsYourRefrigerator.online) is a non-commercial, personal homelab website operated by @Trollmeneerr (Email: fridge@isyourrefrigerator.online). We take your digital privacy seriously and follow the principle of complete data minimization (Art. 5(1)(c) GDPR).

● 0 Cookies / 0 Telemetry

2. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We do not use tracking cookies, analytics services (e.g. Google Analytics), advertising networks, or fingerprinting scripts. All fonts and assets are hosted locally on our own server to prevent unauthorized third-party data transfers.

3. Server Logs & Processing (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)

When you visit this website or connect to our dedicated game server instances, standard technical connection metadata is temporarily processed in local server log files (such as IP address, date/time stamp, requested resource, and user-agent). This processing is strictly necessary for legitimate interests to ensure network security, prevent DDoS abuse, and maintain service stability. Log entries are automatically purged after 14 days.

4. Your Rights Under the GDPR (Articles 15–21)

Under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation, you have full statutory rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access (Art. 15): Inquire whether and what personal data is processed.
  • Right to Rectification & Erasure (Art. 16, 17): Request correction or deletion of log records.
  • Right to Restriction & Objection (Art. 18, 21): Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local EU Data Protection Authority (DPA).
